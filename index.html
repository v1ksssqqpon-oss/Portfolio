FAQ

Common Questions

Didn't find your answer? Write to me directly.

How long does development take? +

Depends on complexity. A basic bot: 3–5 days. A full Mini App with backend: 10–14 days. An enterprise ecosystem: 3–6 weeks. I always give realistic estimates before starting.

Do you provide hosting/deployment? +

Yes. I deploy to your server (VPS/VDS) or can recommend and set up a suitable server. Everything is Dockerized for easy management and scaling.

What is the payment structure? +

Typically 50% upfront, 50% on delivery. For large projects we can split into 3 milestones. I accept crypto (USDT, TON, BTC) and bank transfer.

Will I own the source code? +

Absolutely. After final payment, all source code, documentation and access credentials are fully transferred to you. No licensing restrictions.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Yes. All packages include a free support period (bugs only). Extended monthly support retainers are available for updates, new features and monitoring.
